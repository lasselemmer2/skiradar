Safety notes & liability

Mountains are not a playground. Please read before use.

Not an avalanche warning. The avalanche views in MetaSnow do not replace the official avalanche bulletin. The “avalanche calculator” is an experimental, automatic model based on weather forecasts and can significantly under- or overestimate the danger. The EAWS levels only show the daily maximum per region, without danger spots and avalanche problems, and may be outdated.

Anyone going away from secured slopes reads the official bulletin beforehand, has the necessary training and emergency equipment (transceiver, shovel, probe) and assesses the situation on site themselves. Official bulletins:

  • EAWS – overview of all European warning services
  • Avalanche report Tyrol · South Tyrol · Trentino
  • SLF – Switzerland
  • Avalanche warning service Bavaria
  • Météo-France – Montagne
  • AINEVA – Italy

Weather and snow data

Fresh snow, snow depth, temperature, wind, clouds and sun are forecasts from weather models or derived from them. They are not 100 % reliable and can differ strongly and quickly in the mountains.

Slopes, lifts and routes

Slopes, lifts and routes come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Only the ski resort can tell whether a slope or lift is open. Ski routes, freeride and touring sections are not secured. Closures and signs on site always take precedence.

Tracker

Speed, distance and vertical metres are estimates from GPS and barometer. MetaSnow is not an emergency or locating device. Recording uses noticeable battery.

Prices and driving times

Ski pass, toll and fuel prices and driving times are researched or estimated and without guarantee.
