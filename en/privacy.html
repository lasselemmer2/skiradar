Privacy policy

for the MetaSnow app (iOS/iPadOS) · As of 26 November 2026

In short: No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. Your ski days stay on your device. For the map and weather to work, the app fetches data from the services listed below – these see your IP address and the requested location or map area.

2. Data on your device

Ski days (tracker): When you tap “Start ski day”, the app stores GPS positions, altitude, speed, air pressure changes (barometer) and the detected type of movement (e.g. walking, car) only locally on your device. They are not transmitted to me. You can delete individual ski days in the app; deleting the app removes everything. Depending on your iOS settings, the files may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup.

Settings and cache: map tiles, weather data, slope data and your settings (e.g. home location for driving times) are cached locally to speed things up.

3. Permissions

Location (“While Using the App”): to show you on the map, load weather for your location, calculate routes from your location and – only during a recording you started – record your ski day, even when the device is locked. iOS indicates this during recording. Motion & Fitness: barometer and type of movement during a recording, to tell lifts, runs and car rides apart. You can revoke both permissions at any time in the iOS settings.

4. Data fetched from third parties

For the map, weather, search and routes, the app fetches content directly from the following services. For technical reasons they receive your IP address, device/browser information (user agent) and the content of the request, i.e. coordinates or map areas (e.g. for the weather at your location) or your search term. The app does not send names, accounts or device identifiers. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the function you use) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working map).

Where a service is based outside the EU/EEA (e.g. USA), your IP address may be transferred to that country. The privacy policies of the respective providers apply. I do not operate any server that receives or stores your data.

5. App Store and Apple

When you download and update, Apple processes data according to Apple’s privacy policy. If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in iOS, I receive anonymised crash reports and usage statistics from Apple.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Since I do not store any personal data about you, this mainly concerns the data on your device, which you can delete yourself. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the one responsible for me, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

7. Changes

If the app changes, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
