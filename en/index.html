MetaSnow

3D snow map of the Alps, piste routing and ski tracker for iPhone and iPad. (Formerly: SkiRadar.)

What does the app do?

  • Snow radar: fresh snow, snow depth, temperature, wind and clouds for every ski resort in the Alps on 3D terrain – with a timeline for the coming days.
  • Slopes & lifts: all slopes in the Alps as sharp lines, stored offline in the app.
  • Route from A to B across slopes and lifts, optionally with ski routes, touring ascents or cross-country trails.
  • Weather right here: long-press the map – forecast for exactly this spot and this altitude.
  • Ski tracker: start a ski day, phone in your pocket – the app detects runs, lifts and ascents on its own.
Important: MetaSnow is not an avalanche warning. Weather and snow data are forecasts without guarantee. Please read the safety notes.

FAQ

How do I change the language?

MetaSnow is available in English, German, French and Italian and follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for the app only: in the “Info” tab tap “Language” – this opens MetaSnow’s iOS settings, where you choose under “Language”.

The tracker doesn’t record when my iPhone is locked.

Go to Settings › MetaSnow › Location, choose “While Using the App” and turn on “Precise Location”, then begin the ski day in the app with “Start ski day”. While recording, iOS shows a blue indicator at the top – then it keeps running while locked. Low Power Mode can reduce accuracy.

I’d like to try the app without a ski day of my own.

In the “Ski days” tab, tap “View demo ski day”.

The map stays empty or loads no weather.

The map needs an internet connection. Slopes and lifts are built in; weather and satellite images come from the internet. “Reload weather data” in the map menu helps after interrupted downloads.

A slope is missing or wrong.

Slopes come from OpenStreetMap. You can report or fix errors there directly: openstreetmap.org/fixthemap. The app picks up changes with the next data update (weekly).

Can I bring my ski days to Strava or Garmin?

Yes. In the “Ski days” tab open a day → menu (⋯) → “Export for Strava & Garmin (FIT)”. The file uses the sport “Alpine skiing”, each run as its own lap, altitude from GPS and barometer; lift rides don’t count towards distance. For Strava, upload the file at strava.com/upload/select (the Strava app itself can’t import files). A GPX export is available too.

What happens to my data?

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your ski days stay on your device. Details: privacy policy.
